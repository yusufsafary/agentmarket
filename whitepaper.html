1. Introduction

Agent Market is a night market on BNB Chain built for Binance Agentic Wallet agents. It combines a collectible NFT, the Stall, with a fixed supply token, $AMKT, distributed through a public presale and through claims reserved for Stall holders. Every rule of the market, who can mint, what a mint costs, how the presale converts, and what a holder can claim, is enforced by four public smart contracts. This paper describes those rules exactly as the contracts enforce them.

2. Design principles

Agent first. A Binance Agent ID (.ai) is the market pass. It is read from the public SPACE ID registry, and it sets the price of a wallet's first Stall.

On chain rules. Prices, caps, and claim amounts live in the contracts, not on this website. Any interface, this site, an agent, or a block explorer, reads the same numbers.

No held funds. Mint payments, claim fees, and presale proceeds move to the project wallet inside the same transaction that creates them. No contract in the market keeps a spendable BNB balance.

Public by default. Contract source code is published and matched on Sourcify for all four contracts, and every address is printed in the footer of every page.

3. The Stall NFT

The Stall is a transferable NFT on BNB Chain with a fixed edition of 12,000. Minting is open to any wallet, in the browser or through an agent, up to 25 Stalls per wallet. Prices are computed on chain by the contract and rise by tier:

The free first mint is not a discount code. The contract checks the minting wallet against the Agent ID registry at mint time: hold an Agent ID and the first Stall costs 0 BNB, hold none and it costs 0.0003 BNB.

4. The $AMKT token

$AMKT is a fixed supply token. 10,000,000,000 $AMKT were created at deployment and no function can create more. The supply is allocated as follows:

Public presale1,000,000,000 · 10%
Early holder claim pool300,000,000 · 3%
Presale end holder claim pool300,000,000 · 3%
Liquidity and market operations7,100,000,000 · 71%
Legacy presale contract, locked1,300,000,000 · 13%

A note on the locked line: the first presale contract was replaced before the market opened. The $AMKT it holds cannot be withdrawn by anyone, including the project, and never circulates. The balance is public on chain for anyone to confirm.

5. Presale mechanics

The presale sells at most 1,000,000,000 $AMKT at a fixed rate of 20,000,000 $AMKT per 1 BNB. It runs for exactly 14 days from its on chain start. Buyers pay in BNB, or in USDT converted at the BNB price reported by the Chainlink BNB/USD feed on BNB Chain. Purchases are recorded by the presale contract at purchase time. Purchased $AMKT is not transferred during the sale: buyers claim it after the presale ends, from the same contract, in one claim per wallet.

6. Holder claims

Every Stall carries 25,000 $AMKT of claim value, and the market runs the holder claim twice, so a Stall held through both rounds is worth 50,000 $AMKT in total.

Early holder claim, open now. The AgentMarketClaim contract pays 25,000 $AMKT for every Stall the claiming wallet holds at claim time, once per wallet, for a claim fee of 0.000241 BNB. The fee moves to the project wallet in the same transaction. A wallet that has claimed cannot claim again from this contract, even if it later receives more Stalls.

Presale end holder claim. When the presale ends, the presale contract opens its own holder claim on the same terms, 25,000 $AMKT per Stall held, once per wallet, with no fee. It runs independently of the early claim.

7. Money flow

Follow one mint and one claim and the pattern is the same: value moves once, in the transaction itself. A mint payment reaches the project wallet in the mint transaction. A claim fee reaches the project wallet in the claim transaction. A presale purchase reaches the project wallet in the purchase transaction. Claim pools are the one deliberate exception: $AMKT reserved for holders sits in the claim contracts, where no one, including the project, can withdraw it. It can only leave as a holder claim.

8. Swap and liquidity

The main page carries a swap console routed through the PancakeSwap V2 router. BNB and USDT trade there today. No $AMKT pool exists yet: the pool is created after the presale ends, and the console reads the chain for it and enables $AMKT trading automatically once the pool exists. Prices in the console are quoted by the router at the moment of the quote, with the slippage the visitor selects.

9. Holder launchpad

Agent Market is also a launchpad in waiting. When the launchpad opens, Stall holders get access: the Stall is the ticket, and holding is verified on chain at the moment of entry. Launch terms for future sales will be published on this site before any of them open.

11. Risks

$AMKT and the Stall are crypto assets. Their prices can move to zero, the presale claim opens only after the presale ends, and smart contracts can contain errors even when their source is public.
