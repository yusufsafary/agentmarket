About the project

A night market, run by agents.

agentmarket.exchange · BNB Chain

Agent Market is a night market on BNB Chain, built for Binance Agentic Wallet agents. Its centrepiece is the Stall, a transferable NFT that any wallet can mint, in the browser on this site or through an AI agent acting for its owner. Around the Stall sit a public presale for the market token $AMKT, a holder claim, and a built in swap console.

The market was designed for agent wallets first. A Binance Agent ID (.ai) is the market pass: wallets holding one mint their first Stall free, and every page of the market offers a ready made prompt so an agent can mint, buy, or claim on behalf of its owner.

The Stall

The Stall is an NFT on BNB Chain with a fixed edition of 12,000. Every Stall carries the same collection artwork, a night market stall under a crescent moon, and public metadata served from this domain. A Stall is a normal transferable token: it can be held, sold, or moved between wallets like any other NFT on the chain.

Beyond the artwork, a Stall is a key. Each Stall a wallet holds carries a claim of 25,000 $AMKT, and the holder claim is open now. Stall holders also get access to the Agent Market launchpad when it opens.

How the market works

Mint. Minting happens on the main page, in the browser or through an agent prompt. Wallets holding an Agent ID mint their first Stall for 0 BNB. Every other first mint costs 0.0003 BNB. Stalls 2 to 10 cost 0.0003 BNB each, stalls 11 to 20 cost 0.0005 BNB each, and stalls 21 to 25 cost 0.0007 BNB each. The limit is 25 Stalls per wallet. The tiers are set in the Stall contract, so every interface reads the same prices.

Presale. The $AMKT presale runs on its own page for exactly 14 days once started. The rate is fixed at 20,000,000 $AMKT for every 1 BNB, and the presale sells at most 1,000,000,000 $AMKT in total. Buyers can pay with BNB, or with USDT converted at the live BNB price. Purchases are recorded by the presale contract, and the tokens are claimed after the presale ends.

Claim. The holder claim is open now: Stall holders claim 25,000 $AMKT for every Stall their wallet holds, once per wallet, for a claim fee of 0.000241 BNB, through the AgentMarketClaim contract on the claim page. Presale buyers claim the $AMKT they bought when the presale ends.

Swap. The main page carries a swap console routed through PancakeSwap V2. BNB and USDT trade there today. The console reads the chain for an $AMKT pool and enables $AMKT trading as soon as a pool exists.
