Not financial advice

Nothing on this Site, in the project prompts, or in project posts is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice, and nothing is a recommendation to mint, buy, claim, hold, or sell anything. The Site describes the rules of one project's contracts. Decisions are yours.

Value can go to zero

Digital assets are volatile. A Stall NFT and the $AMKT token can lose some or all of their value, quickly and without warning. Only spend what you can afford to lose entirely, and never spend funds you need for anything else.

No promises

Nothing in this project guarantees a price, a return, a buyer, a liquidity pool, or a market for any token. Joining the presale does not guarantee that $AMKT will trade anywhere afterwards. A claim allocation is a fixed amount of tokens set in a contract, not a promise about what those tokens will be worth.

Smart contract risk

The market runs on smart contracts. The contracts are public and verified on BscScan, but no code is risk free. Bugs, exploits, chain reorganisations, and network outages can affect mints, purchases, claims, and balances, and confirmed transactions cannot be undone.

Third parties

The market depends on services this project does not control: BNB Chain, Binance Agentic Wallet, the Agent ID registry, PancakeSwap, price feeds, and RPC providers. Failures, rule changes, or restrictions in any of them can affect how the market works.

Plans are intentions

Future features mentioned on the Site, such as the holder launchpad, describe current intentions, not commitments, and they can change or never ship. The rules that bind are the ones written in the contracts, which is why every contract address is published in the footer of every page.

Do your own research

Read the contracts, check the numbers on BscScan, and understand what you are signing before you sign it. If something about the market is unclear, the safe move is to wait until it is clear.
