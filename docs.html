How to use the market.

agentmarket.exchange · BNB Chain · Chain ID 56

Before you start

You need a wallet that can hold BNB Chain assets and sign transactions: a browser wallet, or a Binance Agentic Wallet driven by your agent. Keep a small amount of BNB in it for gas and, where they apply, mint prices and the claim fee. An Agent ID (.ai) is optional, but it makes your first Stall free and it is how the market recognises agent wallets. Everything on this site runs on BNB Chain, chain ID 56: if your wallet is on another network, the site will ask it to switch.

Mint a Stall

In the browser. Open the main page, connect your wallet with the button in the top bar, choose how many Stalls to mint in the mint console, and confirm the transaction. The console shows the exact price before you sign, because it reads the price from the Stall contract itself. Wallets holding an Agent ID see 0 BNB for their first Stall.

Through your agent. Copy the agent prompt from the Agent ID section of the main page, or hand your agent the skill file at agentmarket.exchange/agentmarket-skill.md. The agent reads the price with the contract, previews the call, and mints to your wallet after your approval.

Limits that apply to everyone: at most 25 Stalls per wallet, priced by tier: first Stall 0 BNB with an Agent ID or 0.0003 BNB without one, Stalls 2 to 10 at 0.0003 BNB, 11 to 20 at 0.0005 BNB, and 21 to 25 at 0.0007 BNB.

Agent ID

An Agent ID is a .ai name registered in the SPACE ID registry on BNB Chain. Agent Market never issues Agent IDs and never asks for your keys: it only reads the registry to check whether the minting wallet holds one. Use the checker in the Agent ID section of the main page to see what your connected wallet holds. If the checker finds no Agent ID, the first mint simply costs 0.0003 BNB like any other wallet.

Join the presale

Open the presale page, connect your wallet, and choose your lane. Paying with BNB buys at the fixed rate of 20,000,000 $AMKT per 1 BNB. Paying with USDT converts your amount at the live BNB price first, then applies the same rate. Your purchase is recorded on chain immediately, and the presale page shows your position and your claimable amount. Purchased $AMKT is claimed after the presale ends, not during the sale. The presale ends 14 days after its on chain start; the countdown on the presale page is read from the contract.

Claim $AMKT

Holder claim, open now. If your wallet holds Stalls, open the claim page, connect the wallet that holds them, and claim. You receive 25,000 $AMKT for every Stall in that wallet, once per wallet, for a claim fee of 0.000241 BNB. Claiming from a different wallet than the one holding your Stalls pays nothing, and a wallet that already claimed cannot claim again from this contract.

Presale buyer claim. After the presale ends, the claim page and the presale page open the buyer claim: connect the wallet you bought with and claim the full $AMKT amount recorded for it, once, with no fee.

Second holder claim. At the same moment, the presale contract opens a second holder claim of 25,000 $AMKT per Stall held, once per wallet, with no fee. It is separate from the early claim: holding through both rounds pays 50,000 $AMKT per Stall in total.

Agents claim the same way through the claim skill file at agentmarket.exchange/claim-skill.md.

Swap

The swap console on the main page routes through PancakeSwap V2. Pick a pair, enter an amount, and the console quotes the other side from the router before you sign, with your choice of 0.5%, 1%, or 3% slippage. BNB and USDT trade today. $AMKT appears in the console automatically once its pool is created after the presale; until then the $AMKT side stays closed and says why.

For agents

Two skill files describe the whole market in machine readable form: agentmarket-skill.md for mint and presale, and claim-skill.md for claims. The core contract calls behind the site are: Stall costFor(address, quantity) to price a mint and mint(quantity) payable to execute it; Presale buyWithBNB() payable and buyWithUSDT(amount) to buy, and claim() after the presale ends; AgentMarketClaim claimableFor(address) to read a holder position and claim() payable with the fee to execute it. Always preview a call and show your owner the price or fee before executing.

Troubleshooting

The site says wrong network. Switch the wallet to BNB Chain, chain ID 56. The connect flow offers the switch automatically.

My first mint is not free. The free first mint needs an Agent ID in the connected wallet itself. An Agent ID held in a different wallet does not apply.

The claim says already claimed. Each claim contract pays a wallet once. If you claimed the early claim already, the second holder claim at presale end is a different contract and is still ahead of you.

The presale claim is closed. Buyer claims and the second holder claim open when the presale ends. The countdown on the presale page shows the exact moment, read from the contract.

A number on the site looks stale. The boards read the chain directly and refresh on a timer. If an RPC endpoint lags, the last confirmed value stays on screen rather than a guessed one.
