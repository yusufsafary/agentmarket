01 · MINT A STALL

One wallet, up to 25 Stalls.

Stalls are minted only through your Binance Agentic Wallet agent. There is no wallet connection and no mint button on this page. Give your agent the prompt below and it reads the live price from the contract, pays the exact amount, and mints to your Agentic wallet.

Supply is 12,000 Stalls. A batch that crosses a tier pays the combined price of each tier it crosses. Prices are read on chain by costFor before every mint, so your agent always pays the exact amount.

Agent prompt · mint

You are my Binance Agentic Wallet agent. Mint Agent Market Stalls for me on BNB Chain (chain ID 56).

Stall contract: 0xE3dE7fD0d12cFF4a46d8Bf702Ab593B6f921b308

Steps:
1. Read mintOpen() (selector 0x24bbd049). If it returns false, stop and tell me minting is not open yet.
2. Read mintedBy(my wallet) (selector 0x3cef28d2) and costFor(my wallet, quantity) (selector 0xf31cc4e6) for the quantity I asked for.
3. Call mint(quantity) (selector 0xa0712d68) with value exactly equal to costFor. My first mint is free (0 BNB) if my wallet holds an Agent ID (.ai); otherwise the first mint costs 0.0003 BNB. Mints 2 to 10 cost 0.0003 BNB each, 11 to 20 cost 0.0005 BNB each, 21 to 25 cost 0.0007 BNB each. Maximum 25 Stalls per wallet.
4. Show me the transaction and confirm the Stalls arrived in my wallet.

02 · $AMKT PRESALE

One rate. Two ways to pay.

The presale sells $AMKT at a fixed rate of 1 BNB = 20,000,000 AMKT. Your agent can pay with BNB, or with USDT converted at the live Chainlink BNB/USD price. The presale hardcap is 1,000,000,000 AMKT (50 BNB equivalent) and it runs for 14 days from the moment it starts.

Agent prompt · presale

You are my Binance Agentic Wallet agent. Buy $AMKT in the Agent Market presale on BNB Chain (chain ID 56).

Presale contract: 0xA6Ef01e926eFbD35661e7B1d3E1CBeE360750b16

Steps:
1. Read isOpen() (selector 0x47535d7b). If it returns false, stop and tell me the presale is not open.
2. To pay with BNB: call buyWithBNB() (selector 0x20f1fc61) with the BNB amount I gave you as the value. Rate: 1 BNB = 20,000,000 AMKT.
3. To pay with USDT: first approve the presale contract to spend my USDT (USDT contract 0x55d398326f99059fF775485246999027B3197955, approve selector 0x095ea7b3), then call buyWithUSDT(usdtAmount) (selector 0xa7c60160). The contract converts USDT at the Chainlink BNB/USD price.
4. Read bought(my wallet) (selector 0x667022fd) and tell me how much AMKT I will be able to claim after the presale ends.

03 · CLAIM

Every Stall carries tokens.

When the presale ends, two claims open on the claim page, both free of any fee and both handled by your Binance Agentic Wallet agent. Presale buyers claim the AMKT they bought. Stall holders claim 25,000 AMKT per Stall, counted from the Stalls in the wallet at claim time. A wallet holding the maximum 25 Stalls claims 625,000 AMKT.

04 · QUESTIONS

Asked at the market.

How do I mint a Stall?

Through your Binance Agentic Wallet agent. Copy the mint prompt above, send it to your agent, and name the quantity. This site never connects to your wallet and never takes payment.

Why is the first mint free?

Agentic wallets that hold a Binance Agent ID (.ai) mint their first Stall for 0 BNB. The contract reads the Agent ID registry on chain. Wallets without an Agent ID pay 0.0003 BNB from the first mint.

When does the presale start and end?

The start is announced here and on the project channels. Once started, the presale runs exactly 14 days and the live board above counts down to the on-chain end time.

When can I claim $AMKT?

Claims open the moment the presale ends. Presale purchases and Stall holder allocations are claimed separately, once each per wallet, with no claim fee.

What does a Stall get me besides the claim?

Stall holders get access to the Agent Market launchpad when it opens. The Stall itself is a transferable NFT on BNB Chain.
