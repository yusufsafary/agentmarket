The token

$AMKT is the token of Agent Market. 10,000,000,000 $AMKT were created once, at deployment, and the contract has no function that can create more. There is no inflation schedule, no team mint, and no hidden reserve: the whole supply sits in the five places listed on this page, and every one of them can be checked on chain.

Where each part sits

Public presale, 1,000,000,000. Held by the presale contract and sold at the fixed rate of 20,000,000 $AMKT per 1 BNB, in BNB or in USDT converted at the live BNB price. Whatever the presale sells is claimed by its buyers after the sale ends.

Early holder claim pool, 300,000,000. Held by the AgentMarketClaim contract and paying out now: 25,000 $AMKT for every Stall a wallet holds, once per wallet. The contract has no withdrawal function for this pool. It can only leave as holder claims.

Presale end holder claim pool, 300,000,000. Held inside the presale contract. When the presale ends it opens a second holder claim on the same terms, 25,000 $AMKT per Stall, with no fee.

Liquidity and market operations, 7,100,000,000. Held by the project wallet. This is the reserve that seeds the $AMKT pool on PancakeSwap after the presale ends and that funds the market's operations. It is also the only allocation that can move before then, and every movement is a public transaction from a published address.

Legacy presale contract, 1,300,000,000. The first presale contract was replaced before the market opened. The $AMKT it holds cannot be withdrawn by anyone, including the project. It never circulates, never sells, and never claims. Count it out of any supply calculation: the effective ceiling is 8,700,000,000.

How $AMKT reaches holders

There are exactly three doors, and all of them are contracts, not discretion. Buy in the presale and claim after it ends. Hold a Stall and claim 25,000 $AMKT per Stall in the early claim, open now. Hold through the presale end and claim another 25,000 $AMKT per Stall in the second holder claim. A Stall held through both rounds carries 50,000 $AMKT. No other issuance exists, for anyone.

Mint payments, the claim fee, and presale proceeds move to the project wallet inside the same transaction that creates them. No contract in the market keeps a spendable BNB balance, and there is no withdrawal step for anyone to wait for.

What is not promised

$AMKT carries no promised yield, no buyback commitment, and no revenue share. Its price, once a pool exists, is set by the market.
